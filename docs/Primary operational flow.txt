6. Data Flow Design
Primary operational flow

Step 1: Intake

User creates vehicle and appraisal.

Step 2: Inspection

User opens inspection form, enters detailed 12-system findings, assigns scores, notes red flags.

Step 3: Condition normalization

System converts inspection results into normalized condition metrics.

Step 4: Comparable analysis

Comparable sales are attached, normalized, and similarity-ranked.

Step 5: Valuation execution

Valuation engine combines condition, comparables, adjustments, and market rules.

Step 6: Confidence scoring

System determines strength of result based on comp quality, data completeness, and condition certainty.

Step 7: Reporting

Appraisal report is generated from structured records.

Step 8: Delivery / retrieval

Client or staff retrieves final report and valuation details.
